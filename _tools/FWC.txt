1 Project Overview
TalentSphere AI is a next-generation AI-powered Human Resource Management System (HRMS)
designed to automate recruitment, employee management, onboarding, payroll, performance
evaluation, and workforce analytics.
The system combines traditional HRMS functionalities with Artificial Intelligence to reduce
manual effort and improve decision making.
2 Objectives
•Automate HR operations
•Implement AI-driven recruitment
•Provide intelligent employee insights
•Support multiple user roles
•Deliver scalable architecture
•Provide responsive web experience
